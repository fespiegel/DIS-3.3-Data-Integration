## file where we collect our ideas 

1. Spring is arriving earlier. 
Base: DWD phenology data (first bloom dates of plants).
Enrichment: DWD temperature and GBIF species observations.
Key: station/region and year.
Hypothesis: the start of spring has shifted over decades. 

2. Tourism vs. events. 
Base: overnight stays from IT.NRW or Destatis.
Enrichment: scraped event calendars (Carnival, Gamescom, trade fairs) and weather.
Hypothesis: big events lift hotel stays more than good weather does. 

3. Flight delays vs. weather at German airports. 
Base: open flight data (check OpenSky’s terms).
Enrichment: airport weather reports (METAR).
Hypothesis: wind, fog and snow explain most delays. 

4. Do birds arrive earlier when springs get warmer? 
Base: GBIF species observations (check each dataset’s licence).
Enrichment: DWD temperature.
Key: region and year.
Hypothesis: first sightings of swifts or cuckoos shift with spring temperature. 

5. Are songs getting shorter? 
Base: MusicBrainz (CC0) for track lengths and release years.
Enrichment: Wikipedia or Wikidata for genre and chart data.
Key: recording or artist ID.
Hypothesis: average track length fell over the streaming era. 

6. Sugar, salt and Nutri-Score around the world. 
Base: Open Food Facts (ODbL).
Enrichment: World Bank or WHO obesity and health indicators.
Key: ISO country code.
Hypothesis: products sold in countries with higher obesity rates have higher sugar content.

7. KI und Arbeitslosigkeit	Gibt es einen Zusammenhang zwischen der Verbreitung von KI und der Arbeitslosenquote?	Länder mit einer höheren KI-Nutzungsquote weisen eine niedrigere Arbeitslosenquote auf.
   
8.  AI and electricity.
Base: Our World in Data electricity mix or SMARD data.
Enrichment: data centre locations from OpenStreetMap or Wikidata.
Key: country or region.
Hypothesis: regions with many data centres show higher electricity demand growth. Be careful with causal claims here, which is a good limitation to discuss in Q&A.

9. Do model releases move GitHub?
Base: GitHub API stars and commits of major AI repositories.
Enrichment: release dates of models, scraped from Wikipedia.
Key: repository and date.
Hypothesis: star growth spikes in the weeks after major releases.

10. Internet access vs. press freedom: Base: World Bank. Enrichment: scraped Freedom House or Reporters Without Borders scores.

11. Housing and rents: Base: Destatis or Eurostat. Enrichment: scraped city data (students, tourism).
