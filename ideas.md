## file where we collect our ideas 

1. Spring is arriving earlier, and plants and birds follow the temperature.
Base: DWD phenology data (first bloom dates of plants) and GBIF observations of swifts and cuckoos (first sightings).
Enrichment: DWD temperature (March-April mean).
Key: region (federal state or natural region) and year.
Hypothesis: the start of spring has shifted over decades, and first bird sightings shift with spring temperature.

Datanases:  
DWD https://dwd-geoportal.de/ | https://opendata.dwd.de/  
GovData (for city specific plans concerning climate) https://www.govdata.de/ 

2. Tourism vs. events. 
Base: overnight stays from IT.NRW or Destatis.
Enrichment: scraped event calendars (Carnival, Gamescom, trade fairs) and weather.
Hypothesis: big events lift hotel stays more than good weather does. 

3. Flight delays vs. weather at German airports. 
Base: open flight data (check OpenSky’s terms).
Enrichment: airport weather reports (METAR).
Hypothesis: wind, fog and snow explain most delays. 

4. Are songs getting shorter? 
Base: MusicBrainz (CC0) for track lengths and release years.
Enrichment: Wikipedia or Wikidata for genre and chart data.
Key: recording or artist ID.
Hypothesis: average track length fell over the streaming era. 

5. KI und Arbeitslosigkeit	Gibt es einen Zusammenhang zwischen der Verbreitung von KI und der Arbeitslosenquote?	Länder mit einer höheren KI-Nutzungsquote weisen eine niedrigere Arbeitslosenquote auf.
   
6.  AI and electricity.
Base: Our World in Data electricity mix or SMARD data.
Enrichment: data centre locations from OpenStreetMap or Wikidata.
Key: country or region.
Hypothesis: regions with many data centres show higher electricity demand growth. Be careful with causal claims here, which is a good limitation to discuss in Q&A.

7. Movies: Ratings vs. budget, genre or release month (IMDb non-commercial datasets plus Wikipedia box-office lists).
